How it works

  1. Press ⌘ ⇧ 6, or pick "Sum a Region" from the menu bar icon.
  2. Drag a rectangle over the numbers or table.
  3. Read the totals under each column. Click one to copy it.
  4. Use the panel to copy the table or text. Press Esc when you're done.

First launch

TallyShot is free and not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.
  1. Open the downloaded TallyShot.dmg and drag TallyShot into Applications.
  2. Open TallyShot from Applications. macOS shows "TallyShot.app" Not Opened. Click Done, not Move to Bin.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security, find the TallyShot message, and click Open Anyway. Enter your password.
  4. Open TallyShot again and click Open.
  5. When asked, allow Screen Recording, then quit TallyShot from the menu bar icon and reopen it. It only reads the area you select.

Installed with the Terminal command? Skip steps 1 to 4.
